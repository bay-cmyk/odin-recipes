Spring Recipes

Description

Pinsa is a typical italian dish, the sister of pizza one could say, very popular in Rome.

This delicious version will blow your mind!

Ingredients

  • Store bought pinsa
  • 1 courgette
  • 1 medium-sized potato/li>
  • 1 mozzarella
  • Some store bought pesto

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven at 200 degrees Celsius
  2. Cut the courgette and potato into round slices (around 2 mm wide)
  3. Put a sheet of parchment paper on a baking tray, lay the vegetables slices on it without overlapping them and bake them until golden. Flip them halfway through
  4. When the courgette and the potato are almost done, pop the pinsa in the oven as well to warm up
  5. In the meantime, chop the mozzarella. You don't need to be precise, it will melt!
  6. Take the pinsa and the vegetables out of the oven
  7. Lay some pesto on the pinsa, then the courgette and potato slices and homogeneously sprinkle the mozzarella on top
  8. Put the pinsa back in the oven and bake until the mozzarella is properly melted and the pinsa in nice and gold
  9. Cut into slices
Et voila! Enjoy you freshly baked vegetarian pinsa!
