Summer Recipes

Description

What's better than a hot bowl of ramen in a cold winter night? Here's how I make it when I really crave it but I'm too lazy to do everything from scratch.

Ingredients

For the broth and the noodles:

  • Miso paste
  • Hot water
  • Noodles

For the toppings:

  • 1 egg
  • Canned corn
  • 1 or 2 slices of pork - or tofu
  • A bunch of seaweed snacks
  • A green of some sort, this could be a pak choi, some spinach, etc...
  • Other toppings of choice

Steps

Do this the day before!

Prepare the egg by boiling it. Cover it in soy sauce (you can dilute it with a little bit of water - things are expensive these days) and let it sit in in the fridge until you're ready to make your ramen.

Tip!

Lay a piece of paper towel over the soy sauce! This will prevent the part of the egg that is not covered by the liquid, to brown less than the rest.

  1. Bring some water to boil and cook the noodles in it for a couple of minutes - or follow the package instructions
  2. In the meantime, prepare your toppings:
    • Cook the protein of your choice and set it aside
    • Pan fry the corn until it's nice and roasted
    • Take the marinated egg out of the fridge and cut it in half
    • Boil your spinach or pan fry your pak choi
    • Prepare the rest of your toppings of choice
    • Cut the seaweed sheets into thin slices
  3. Take the noodles out of the water and pour some of the boiling water in a bowl with the miso paste and stir well
  4. Now it's time to assemble! Add the noodles to the broth and top it with all of your delicious toppings

Your ramen bowl is ready to be slurped! Enjoy!
