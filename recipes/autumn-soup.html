Fried Halloumi

Description

A vegetarian and delicious dish for the perfect autumn lunch.

Ingredients

  • Halloumi
  • A can of chickpeas
  • A quarter of a pumpkin
  • 100g of fresh baby spinach
  • Tahini sauce
  • 1 slice of lemon
  • 1 teaspoon of honey
  • 1 clove of garlic

Steps

  1. Chop the pumpkin into cubes and throw it in a pan with the garlic and some oil.
  2. Add a pinch of salt, cover the pan with a lid and cook for about 15 minutes.
  3. Mix the tahini sauce with 2 tablespoons of water, the honey, the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  4. Rinse the chickpeas and add them to the pumpkin, with the spinach.
  5. Pan fry the vegetable for a couple of minutes and then set aside.
  6. Slice the halloumi and pan fry it in a pan with a little bit of oil until the outside is nice and crispy.
  7. Serve the halloumi on top of the vegetables and drizzle them with the sauce.

    8. And you're all done! Enjoy!
