Baked pumpkin

Description

Ingredients

  • Hokkaido pumpkin
  • 250g sausage
  • 3 medium-sized onions
  • 200g of melting cheese
  • Sage
  • Some bread to serve

Steps

  1. Clean the outside of the pumpkin thoroughly.
  2. Bake it in the oven at 220 degrees (Celsius) for about 20-30 minutes.
  3. In the meantime, break the sausage into small pieces and cook it in a pan until nice and crispy.
  4. Chop the onion into thin stripes and cook it in a pan on low heat until it's caramelised.
  5. Once the pumpkin is soft but still keeping its shape, cut the top off and remove the seeds.
  6. Sprinkle some salt in the pumpkin then start filling it:
    • First, lay some cheese on the bottom.
    • Then half of the onion and the sausage on top.
    • Add some sage.
    • And finally, cover with some more cheese.
  7. Put its "lid" back on top and bake for another 20 minutes.

    8. Et voila! You're pumpkin is ready! Enjoy it with some bread on the side and be careful: it's going to be hot!
