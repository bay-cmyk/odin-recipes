Sweet and sour chickpeas

Description

A delicious and vegan comfort food that makes the perfect dinner for a rainy summer night. The combination of sweet and sour creates that bliss spot that will keep you wanting to make this.

Ingredients

For the veggies:

  • Canned chickpeas
  • Red bell peppers
  • Red onion
  • Olive oil

For the sauce:

  • Ketchup
  • Soy sauce
  • Honey
  • Vinegar
  • Paprika
  • Tabasco (if you want it spicy!)
  • Corn starch
  • Water

Steps

  1. Take the bell pepper as it is and roast it in the air fryer at 200 degrees for 30 minutes, flipping it halfway through.

    2. Tip!

    You can also do this in the oven, the time needed to cook the bell pepper might differ but the result will be the same!

  2. Once the pepper is fully cooked, let it cool completely in the air fryer or in a bowl, covered with some cling film.
  3. Peel the bell pepper and remove the stem and the seeds, then cut it in small stripes.
  4. Chop the onion and throw it in a pan with a bit of olive oil.
  5. Once the onion is cooked, add the bell peppers and turn the heat on low.
  6. Strain the chickpeas and rinse them from their water, then add them to the pan with the rest of the vegetables.

    7. Tip!

    You can keep the chickpeas water and use it instead of eggs for a vegan cake! It's called: aquafaba.

  7. Quickly grab a small bowl and make the sauce:
    • Add a little bit of water to a teaspoon of cornstarch and mix it well
    • Add two tablespoons of ketchup
    • Add one tablespoon of soy sauce and one of vinegar
    • Add one tablespoon of honey
    • Add a teaspoon of paprika - and some tabasco if you like the spice!
  8. Pour the sauce in the pan with the vegetables and turn the heat on medium-high
  9. Stir until the sauce has thickened enough

Et voila! You've made Sweet and sour chickpeas! Enjoy this recipe with some fluffy rice and top it with some sesame seeds.
