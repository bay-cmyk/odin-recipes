Dal Palak

Description

Those who say that vegetarian dishes are bland clearly has never tried these aromatic lentils and spinach in a creamy coconut sauce! A slightly spicy and buttery naan is the perfect companion for this dish, too!

Ingredients

  • Canned lentils
  • Fresh baby spinach
  • Coconut milk
  • Curry mix
  • Chilly flakes
  • Cumin
  • Butter
  • Store bought naan

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven at 200 degrees Celsius
  2. Finely chop the baby spinach
  3. Put some oil in a pan and pan fry the spinach until their volume becomes manageable

    4. Note:

    It will look like it's a lot of spinach at the beginning but, I promise, it will reduce a lot very quickly.

  4. Rinse the lentils and add them to the pan
  5. Add the coconut milk and the spice mix

    6. Tip!

    You can use a store-bought curry mix but I recommend also adding a bunch of spices, i.e. cumin, tumeric, paprika, pepper, and even some powdered coriander if you like it!

  6. Let it simmer for a bit, until the coconut milk is no longer watery
  7. Meanwhile, melt a little bit of butter and add some cumin and chilly flakes to it, mix well
  8. Coat both sides of the naan with the spicy butter and put it in the oven to golden. Flip it and golden the other side
  9. Serve the lentils and spinach in a bowl with the bread on the side

Enjoy!
